* Bug list according to Claude
This document presents a list of bugs found in Chezweb, as Claude sees
them. Each bug has a title, a short description, and how it was fixed.

** Misplaced status line
On a single window pane, the status line appears in the middle of the
pane, as there were two windows. To test this bug, Mens van der Lus
preserved a fossilized version of Chezweb in ~/mysand/xbug.

** False enumerate environment in LaTeX
When I paste a reference to an Org mode file, then I press M-q,
sometimes the year goes to a new line, and the LaTeX converter
considers it as an enumerate environment. In Org mode, the stranded
year appears in red. In LaTeX, it can't be seen anywhere.
